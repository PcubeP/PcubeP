## 👋 Hi, I'm Pranit

I'm a **Full Stack Cloud Native & AI/ML Engineer at SAP Labs India**, based in Bengaluru, where I ship production generative-AI and RAG services on SAP BTP and run a 10-person squad as Scrum Master. My degree is in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (B.E., CGPA **8.9/10**) — but what I actually care about comes down to two kinds of things, and they're more connected than they look.

**AI that respects you.** I'm a little obsessed with privacy-first, *on-device* AI — models that run entirely in your browser, with no servers, no uploads, and no API keys. Your photos, your resume, your data: they never leave your machine.

**AI that includes people.** I'm fluent in both **Indian Sign Language (ISL)** and **American Sign Language (ASL)**, and accessibility isn't a checklist item for me — it's a throughline. I think the best technology signs back.

In between, I ship a *lot* of small, polished web apps, and once in a while I build something purely for the joy of it — like a procedurally animated short film on an HTML canvas.

> **My ethos:** AI should run where you are, keep what's yours, and leave no one out.
